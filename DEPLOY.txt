DEPLOY CHECKLIST
================

Everything here sits flat at the site root. Do not nest it in /public
(that folder only applies to framework projects, not plain static sites).

1. Deploy this folder once to get your URL.
2. Open index.html, find SITE_URL, replace all 5 occurrences with that
   URL, no trailing slash. Example: https://layla-birthday.vercel.app
3. Deploy again.
4. Re-scrape the link at https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
   so WhatsApp picks up the preview image.
5. Send the link to yourself first and confirm the card looks right.

The preview image will not appear until step 2 is done. Link scrapers
cannot resolve relative paths, so og:image must be a full absolute URL.
